nod.

Your agent asks. You nod.

  1. 1

    Your agent asks. When an AI agent wants to spend SOL, it sends a request to your phone. It never touches a key.

  2. 2

    Your phone shows the truth. Nod simulates the transaction and shows what would really leave your wallet, whatever the agent says it is.

  3. 3

    You nod. Your wallet signs and the agent gets the signature. Reject it, or pause everything, and the agent is told to stop.

Download for Android APK, Android 7 or newer. Works with Solana devnet.

Your phone may ask you to allow installs from your browser. Checksums are in SHA256SUMS.
